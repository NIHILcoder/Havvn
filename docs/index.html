03 / OPEN

Open to inspection.

MIT-licensed source and changelog on GitHub. Network and storage controls in the app.

What does peer to peer mean here?+

Peer discovery uses external trackers and STUN; search uses selected sources. Connectivity depends on networks and available members. Encryption protects content, but doesn't hide your IP from peers and discovery infrastructure.

07 / BEFORE YOU GO

BEFORE YOU START.

Does my friend have to install Havvn?

Not for a shared file or browser room. Browser guests can chat, talk and watch together. Full file and server management is in the desktop app.

Is it really free?

Yes. MIT-licensed, open source, without paid tiers or Havvn accounts.

Can I use it without a VPN?

Yes, but P2P connections don't hide your IP. Havvn doesn't replace a VPN. Its VPN protection works with your VPN and OS settings.

Where should I start?

Download your platform's build. Open a magnet link or .torrent, or create a room and invite your friends.
