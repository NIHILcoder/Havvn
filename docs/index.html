PRIVACY YOU CAN INSPECT

Your data.
In your hands.

Open source and clear controls. See how the app connects to the network and where your data stays.

◎

A clearer network picture

Direct HTTPS IP, system web proxy and tunnel routes are shown separately. An exit IP's country isn't presented as your location.

⏸

Protection when a VPN drops

VPN protection checks local routes and suspends transfers and room networking. Native engine tunnel binding depends on the available configuration.

◇

Your keys stay with you

Secrets use OS-backed protection; chat history is stored encrypted. In E2E rooms, files are encrypted before being shared.

↳

No application servers doesn't mean no external connections. Peer discovery uses trackers and STUN; search uses the sources you choose. Connectivity depends on networks and available peers; member relaying is possible, but cannot guarantee access across every network.

Linux guide ↗
How do I verify my download?

Compare SHA-256 with the checksum in the release. Linux packages include SHA256SUMS-linux.txt. See your release notes for scan results and installer warnings.

Get-FileHash .\Havvn-Setup-<version>.exe -Algorithm SHA256

A matching checksum confirms the file matches the published one; it isn't a security guarantee.

GOOD TO KNOW

No loose ends.

A few answers before you jump in.

Is it really free?

Yes. Havvn is open source under the MIT license. No paid tier, Havvn accounts or telemetry.

Does my friend need to install the app?

Not for receiving a shared file or joining a browser room. Browser guests can chat, talk and watch together. Full file and server management is available in the desktop app.

Does Havvn replace a VPN?

No. P2P connections can expose IP addresses to peers and discovery infrastructure. E2E encryption protects content, not your address. The app's VPN protection works alongside your VPN and OS settings.

Where do search results come from?

You add search providers and RSS feeds. Havvn doesn't bundle indexers for copyrighted material. Use it for content you're allowed to download and distribute.
